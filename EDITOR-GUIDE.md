# Editor guide

## Sign in

1. Open `/admin/` and sign in with the email and password sent to you by the website administrator.
2. If you forget your password, choose **Forgot password?** and follow the email link.
3. Choose **Sign out** when you finish. Your unfinished form is saved in this browser so you can return to it.

## Publish a post

1. Choose a large action card, such as **Post a Sermon** or **Add an Event**.
2. Fill in the required fields. The preview updates as you type. Use **Save draft** if you need to finish later.
3. To add a photo, PDF or audio recording, choose the file field. Photos are reduced before upload; the form shows the accepted size limits.
4. Choose **Publish now**, **Schedule**, or **Hide at** time. Times are East Africa Time (UTC+3).
5. Check the message after saving. If the website is live while its background copy is still updating, the dashboard will say so.

Use **Edit** to change a post, **Hide from website** to temporarily remove it, and **Remove from website** to hide it while keeping it recoverable. Contact the administrator if you need an older version restored or an item permanently removed.

## Photos and drafts

Drafts are stored in your browser and in your account's private draft area. Use the same browser to restore a draft saved before a connection or browser interruption. Do not use a shared public computer for sensitive drafts; sign out when finished.

## Need help?

If a save fails, keep the page open and retry when the connection is stable. If the message continues, contact the website administrator and include the post title and the time you tried.
