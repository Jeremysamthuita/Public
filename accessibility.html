Accessibility

AIC Egerton Chapel aims to make this website usable with a range of devices and assistive technologies. Pages include keyboard-accessible navigation, semantic headings, visible focus indicators and responsive layouts where supported by the page.

If you encounter a barrier or need information in another format, contact aicegertonchapel@gmail.com or call +254 799 522 418. Please include the page and the difficulty you experienced so we can investigate.
