A community rooted in faith

Serving our community since April 2008 through worship, outreach, and discipleship.

Church History

AIC Egerton Chapel was started in April 2008 through a merger of two like-minded groups of christians; one from AIC Lochorai and other from Egerton Community Chapel. This is mainstream church run by a Local Church Council (LCC), and held accountable by the DCC, RCC, ACC all the way to the CCC.

The church recognizes the fact that the church's mission as the light of the world and salt of the earth is not only to provide the spiritual needs but also to take care of the physical needs of the world in its immediate vicinity as well as those of the world that she does not touch directly.

Over the years, it has grown to become a beacon of faith, hope, and love, impacting countless lives through worship, outreach, and discipleship...

Mission

To Know Christ, experience His power, and be true witnesses of christ in a fallen world according to 2 Timothy 2:2

  1. Help people to know christ
  2. Help believers to experience the power of Christ
  3. Be the ambassadors of Christ on earth
  4. Fulfil the great commission by:
    • Reaching out to the lost
    • Making disciples
    • Teaching to obedience

Vision

To become a beacon of hope as Christ-centered church transforming lives through faith, love, and service, empowering generations to live out the Gospel and impact the world for God's Kingdom.

Motto

Becoming a Beacon of Hope

Values

  • Biblical Foundation - Commitment to Scripture as our ultimate authority
  • Christ-Centered Worship - Glorifying God in spirit and truth
  • Discipleship & Spiritual Growth - Nurturing believers to maturity
  • Community & Fellowship - Building authentic relationships
  • Evangelism & Outreach - Sharing the Gospel locally and globally
  • Integrity & Stewardship - Managing resources with accountability
  • Compassion & Service - Meeting needs with Christ-like love
